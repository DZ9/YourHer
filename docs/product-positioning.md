# Product positioning

YourHer AI is a self-hosted AI companion with personality, memory and conversational continuity. The complete web application is the primary experience; reusable packages are the developer-facing foundation.

The presentation takes inspiration from Character.AI's character-first framing: introduce who the companion is, how its style can be shaped, and what an ongoing conversation feels like before explaining the infrastructure.

Current supported customization is a companion name, feminine/masculine appearance, speaking style and relationship preference. The app supports one configured companion experience per profile. It does not currently offer a character marketplace, public character publishing, multi-character worlds, a social feed or image/video generation; those should not be advertised as implemented.

Official references consulted:

- [Character.AI: About](https://character.ai/about) — creation, connection and exploration as product motivations.
- [Character Definition](https://support.character.ai/hc/en-us/articles/50609183646875-5-Character-Definition) — personality, voice and behavioral consistency.
- [Smarter Memory for Smarter Chats](https://blog.character.ai/memory/) — explaining persistent context in terms of the conversation experience.

YourHer AI is an independent open-source project. Its implementation and product assets do not come from Character.AI; no affiliation or feature parity is claimed.
